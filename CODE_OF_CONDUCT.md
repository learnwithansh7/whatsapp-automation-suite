# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in this project, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards & Expectations

We encourage behaviors that foster a positive collaborative atmosphere:

- **Respect and empathy**: Treat all contributors and users with dignity and kindness.
- **Constructive feedback**: Provide thoughtful, polite, and actionable feedback on code and documentation.
- **Collaboration**: Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- **Responsible usage**: Adhere strictly to anti-spam, privacy, and compliance guidelines. Never use automation tools to send unsolicited communications.

Unacceptable behaviors include harassment, derogatory comments, personal attacks, publishing private information without consent, and any form of abusive conduct.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If you encounter or witness inappropriate conduct, please contact the maintainers privately via GitHub or security contact channels. Maintainers will review and take appropriate and fair action in response.
